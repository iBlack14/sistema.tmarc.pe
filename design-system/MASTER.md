# 🏛️ TMARC Design System (MASTER)
> Powered by UI-UX-PRO-MAX Intelligence

## 🎯 Product Vision
**TMARC Case Tracking** is a premium, high-performance judicial tracking portal. It must convey trust, precision, and authority.

---

## 🎨 Color System (Palette: "Golden Authority")
- **Primary**: `#D4AF37` (Metallic Gold) - Headers, Primary Buttons, Active States.
- **Surface**: `#1A1A1A` (Deep Obsidian) - Main Sidebar, Header Backgrounds.
- **Background**: `#F8F9FA` (Soft Pearl) - Page Content, Form Backgrounds.
- **Accent**: `#2D2D2D` (Steel Gray) - Secondary Containers, Borders.
- **Semantic**:
  - Success: `#4CAF50` (Emerald)
  - Warning: `#FF9800` (Amber)
  - Error: `#F44336` (Crimson)
  - Info: `#2196F3` (Azure)

## ✍️ Typography (Pairing: "Modern Institutional")
- **Headings**: `Outfit` or `Inter` (Bold, 700) - Clean, geometric, authoritative.
- **Body**: `Inter` (Regular, 400) - Optimized for readability in data-heavy views.
- **Monospace**: `JetBrains Mono` - For tracking codes and numbers.

## 📏 Spacing & Rhythm (8dp System)
- **Base Unit**: `8px`
- **Container Padding**: `24px` (Desktop), `16px` (Mobile)
- **Component Gap**: `12px` or `16px`

## ✨ Visual Effects
- **Style**: **Professional Minimalism** with subtle **Glassmorphism** for overlays.
- **Shadows**: Soft, multi-layered shadows (No hard borders).
- **Transitions**: `250ms ease-out` for all interaction states.

---

## 🏗️ Component Standards

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary**: Gold background, black text, rounded-lg (8px), scale-on-press.
- **Secondary**: Ghost style with gold border, black text.

### 2. Cards
- White background, 2px border-gold (top or left), soft shadow, transition on hover.

### 3. Data Tables
- Header: Dark obsidian with gold text.
- Row: Alternating subtle pearl background.

---

## 🚫 Anti-Patterns (Avoid)
- No emojis as primary icons.
- No pure black (`#000`) for large surfaces; use Obsidian (`#1A1A1A`).
- No gray-on-gray text.
- No instant state changes (must have transitions).
